About the property

Description property

Moran Auctioneers are delighted to present No. 28 Knights Park, a spacious four-bedroom detached bungalow ideally situated in a well-established Tesidential area within easy walking distance of Castlebar Town Centre.

The property offers bright, well-proportioned accommodation comprising a generous Living Room, Fitted Kitchen, Separate Utility, Four bedrooms (one Ensuite) and a family Bathroom. externally, the property benefits from a lawn and tarmacadam driveway to the front, together with a private, South-facing rear garden.

Location:-

Knights Park enjoys a highly convenient location within close proximity to local Schools, Shops, Mayo University Hospital and all the amenities of Castlebar Town Centre, making this an attractive opportunity for those seeking a spacious family home in an established Residential setting.
